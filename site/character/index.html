A death costs 10% of a level

In waystones a death takes 10% of the XP needed for your next level. The bar stops at 0%; you never lose a level.

Upper bound, not a safety verdict

  • Measured against a BASE monster of that area level. Rares, uniques and map mods hit much harder and are not modelled, so treat this as an upper bound, not a safety verdict.
  • Your weakest side is chaos: a 3,808 hit kills you. Other damage types have more room.
  • Low (12× or more) and high (under 6×) risk, and the 80% XP limit, are this project’s judgement, not game constants.

How much harder does a specific rare, unique or map modifier hit?

Monster damage multipliers per rarity and per map modifier are not in the data used here. Only the base monster figure for the area level is derived, so rare, unique and boss hits are not scored.
