    Old advice

  • Matriarch and Patriarch Halls

    ⚠ Unverified
    Old advice
    Spawned deep in the outer Atlas fog.
    0.5.5
    Spawn much closer to the Ziggurat Refuge, always as a pair, visible as a smooth orange beam in the fog.

  • Pinnacle boss resistances

    Old advice
    Arbiter of Ash, Arbiter of Divinity, Omniphobia and Raven Trickster all had +75% All Elemental Resistances.
    0.5.5
    Ash resists fire and is weak to cold. Divinity has 45% Lightning Resistance and is weak to fire (0% Cold); Omniphobia and Raven Trickster lost All Elemental Resistance.

  • Rakiata's Flow

    ⚠ Unverified
    Old advice
    A near-mandatory chase gem that inverted the +75% boss resistances.
    0.5.5
    Unequip it on Fire or Tri-Elemental (Trinity) builds against Arbiter of Divinity: inverting Fire Vulnerability gives it positive Fire Resistance. Lightning builds bring Lightning Exposure or a curse instead.

  • Corrupted Nexus failures

    ⚠ Unverified
    Old advice
    Failing a Nexus, or dying and respawning on its map boss, could stop the Corrupted Boss spawning.
    0.5.5
    A failed Corrupted Nexus can be retried, and dying on the map boss no longer stops the Corrupted Boss spawning.

  • Runes of Aldur and Expedition

    Old advice
    League-only mechanic with no Expedition Tablets; needed Aldur's Saga, which stays Runes of Aldur-only.
    0.5.5

    ⚠ Unverified
    Old advice
    Not covered by this guide.

Trial of Chaos (0.5.5 rework)Spec-sourced, unverifiedPause and resume, a chest every room, 30 rooms on one Ultimatum, which modifiers to take and which wagers.
  • Pause or leave between any two rooms, and resume later.
  • A reward chest spawns after every room.
  • Completion rewards are Currency and Soul Cores only. 17 new Soul Cores were added; attribute-conversion cores went from 20% to 40%.

Take: free or buffs loot

  • Lethal Rares +30 to 100% Rare Monsters.
  • Monster Resistances +10 to 50% resistances; also helps Rakiata's Flow users.
  • Unstoppable Monsters Free for most builds.
  • Shielded Monsters Free for most builds.
  • Blood Globules Take the lowest tier.
  • Stalking Shade Tier 1 to 2 only; avoid it in Soul Core rooms.
  • Chaotic / Toxic Monsters Only if you are Chaos Inoculated or Chaos-capped.

Avoid: run-killers

  • Damaged Defences 20 to 80% less Armour, Evasion and Energy Shield.
  • Reduced Resistances -15 to -45% Elemental Resistances and -5 to -15% maximum at Tier 3 to 5.
  • Reduced Recovery 20 to 80% less recovery rate.
  • Time Paradox Buffs expire 50 to 200% faster.

Wagers

  • Wager of the Future early: doubles the stack size of every currency reward offered from then on.
  • Wager of the Present late: doubles all pending currency rewards once Divines are pending.
  • Never take Wager of Rarity or Wager of Rerolling while you hold valuable pending currency.

Essence crafting — Perfect and Corrupted Essences8 essencesThe 3-prefix / 3-suffix isolation rule, the Crystallisation Omens, and the high-impact endgame essences.
  • Lesser, normal and greater Essences turn a Magic (2-mod) item into a Rare with one guaranteed mod.
  • Perfect and Corrupted Essences (Hysteria, Delirium, Horror, Insanity, Abyss) replace an existing random modifier on a Rare item.
  • A Rare holds at most 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes, and a Perfect Essence cannot be used if the item already has a modifier from the same family.
  • A prefix Perfect Essence on a Rare with 3 full prefixes always removes one of those prefixes, so your suffixes are safe. The same holds for a suffix Perfect Essence and 3 full suffixes.
  • If the side you are slamming is not full, add Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation (forces a prefix to be removed) or Omen of Dextral Crystallisation (forces a suffix).
  • Vaal Orb Essence crystals that hold cheap Perfect Essences: they can upgrade to the expensive Corrupted ones (Horror, Hysteria).

All common progression mistakes14Each step shows its own warnings — open this for the full list.
  • Pushing map tiers before defenses are ready

    Endgame is a survival check, not just a DPS check — gear resistances and life before chasing higher Waystone tiers.

  • Spreading Atlas points across multiple mechanics

    The good nodes sit deep in each sub-tree. Finish one mechanic before starting another.

  • Ignoring Waystone sustain

    Spending every good Waystone without securing sustain nodes/backups stalls your progression entirely.

  • Rushing T15 when T11 already gives ~95% of loot value

    T15 rarely beats T11 loot. Run the highest tier you clear quickly, and drop down if maps slow you.

  • Skipping Fortress progression

    The Precursor Fortress is your Atlas-point engine, not a side mechanic — don't neglect it in favor of farming loops.

  • Running a mechanic's own tablets while questing itAbyssBreachRitual

    Tablets add more of a mechanic and slow its quest. Finish the quest first, then juice.

  • Taking Atlas trap nodes (no respec)AbyssBreach

    Atlas points can't be refunded. Check the Traps tab on the Atlas page before you spend one.

  • Full-clearing maps before the Atlas is unlocked

    Mechanics are weak without Atlas points. Boss-rush every map (Essences and Shrines only) until the second Arbiter of Divinity kill unlocks the full tree.

  • Running individual Fortress nodes

    Only path to quest objectives. The second Arbiter of Divinity kill finishes the Fortress anyway.

  • Expecting the first Arbiter of Divinity kill to unlock the Atlas

    The first kill doesn't unlock the full tree. Build a second Origin Core from Halls outside the Fortress and kill it again.

  • Killing the boss before finishing a mechanic point map

    On Atlas-point maps, finish the mechanic before the boss, or the point may not count.

  • Running anomaly maps before taking the Lineage Support nodes

    Allocate the anomaly-map Lineage Support drop nodes before any Jado anomaly map — Lineage gems are expensive and the nodes can double the drop.

  • Juicing Waystones while you're still unlocking the Atlas

    Extra mods add little loot while you boss-rush and cost revives. Save Rare Waystones for Powerful Boss nodes.

  • Running Ritual tablets during the Bodach body-part mapsRitual

    While collecting the 5 body parts for The Bodach, run the chosen maps without Ritual tablets — they can get in the way of the quest drop.
